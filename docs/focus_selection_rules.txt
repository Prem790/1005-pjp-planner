PJP FOCUS SELECTION RULES

STATUS
Implemented as a provisional focus-selection stage. A selected set is not a
certificate that actual dates and routes are feasible. That must be checked
by the scheduling/routing stage. No simulator performance is claimed.

POLICY AND CAPACITY TRADE-OFF
The brief says approximately 100 focus accounts but explicitly requires six
normal dealer visits per working day, club outlets twice and other outlets
once. With 25 working days there are 150 nominal visits per SO. Reserving all
club obligations implies 142, 132 and 149 distinct outlets for the supplied
club counts, rather than exactly 100. This baseline follows the explicit call
norm and reports low-evidence capacity-fill selections separately.

Filling nominal capacity leaves no unused normal-day slots as a reserve. Rain,
unavailability, emergent drops and required retries can reduce coverage. We
will measure that trade-off in the simulator instead of claiming that 450
planned visits imply 450 completions. Selecting fewer accounts would require
an explicit alternative to the six-per-day normal plan or the once-per-month
non-club frequency rule; it is not silently substituted here.

SELECTION ORDER
1. Validate the ranking snapshot, workbook hash, dealer universe, current SO,
   membership, parent mapping and the complete November calendar.
2. Preserve every explicitly flagged club outlet and allocate two visits.
   Related club outlets remain separately protected even when they share an
   owner. Do not replace these with a single owner-level visit.
3. Screen known coordinates against an optimistic six-visit-day travel bound:
   direct travel from the SO start plus 6 x 25 minutes must fit 460 minutes.
   This assumes zero other travel. A failure proves that a normal six-visit
   day is impossible under the model; a pass does not prove a feasible tour.
4. If a protected club cannot fit even that bound, fail visibly for review.
   Never silently remove it to make the numbers work.
5. Select optional outlets in the unchanged commercial rank order until the
   nominal monthly visit budget is filled. Defer an optional outlet if its
   optimistic travel bound fails or its owner is already represented.
6. Unknown locations stay eligible under the supplied travel fallbacks and
   receive a review flag. No invented coordinates or external API is used.
7. A zero-score dealer can fill remaining capacity, but is clearly flagged.
   This does not mean verified new-business potential. A never-purchased
   account is separately identified; its commercial value remains uncertain.
8. Give every selected and deferred dealer an explicit decision reason. If
   insufficient eligible outlets remain, report the capacity shortfall and
   retain all club obligations; do not manufacture duplicate dealer visits.

GEOGRAPHY AND NEXT STAGE
The selector records direct travel, normal/rain lower bounds, missing-location
flags and shared-owner flags. It does not yet cluster stops into days or assign
club visit dates. Passing a single-dealer lower bound is necessary but not
sufficient for a joint daily route.

Five unlocated upcountry calls on a rain day would consume 462.5 minutes under
the supplied model, above the 460-minute dealer budget. The route/re-plan stage
must account for this rather than assuming that five calls always fit.

Next: allocate actual dates, separate club visits by at least ten days, group
stops geographically and validate every route. Any repair that changes focus
selection must be logged and the original focus-coverage implications retained.
